# Privacy Policy — ShareSafe

ShareSafe is an offline screenshot redaction tool. **All image processing happens entirely on your device.**

## Data collection
ShareSafe collects no personal data: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no crash reporting.

## Network access
The app does not request `INTERNET` or `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` permissions — it cannot transmit data off the device.

## Photos & files
Images you pick never leave your device. Detection (OCR, faces, barcodes) runs on-device via bundled ML Kit models. Redacted exports go to `Pictures/ShareSafe` or the app you share to.

## Settings
Your "always redact" word list and preferences live in the app's private storage only.

## Google Play data-safety (pre-filled answers)
- Data collected: **None**
- Data shared: **None**
- Data processed on device only: **Yes**
- Encryption in transit: **N/A** (no network)
- Data deletion: app data cleared via system Settings → Apps.
