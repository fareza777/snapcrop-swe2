ShareSafe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

ShareSafe is an offline screenshot redaction tool. All image processing happens entirely on your device.

Data collection

ShareSafe collects no personal data. It has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting service.

Network access

The app does not request the INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. It is technically incapable of transmitting data off your device.

Photos and files

Images you pick stay on-device. Detection (text recognition, face detection, barcode scanning) is performed locally using on-device machine-learning libraries that ship inside the app. Redacted images you choose to save are written to your gallery (Pictures/ShareSafe); images you share leave your device only through the app you share them with.

Settings

Your "always redact" word list and preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage and are never transmitted.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on the project repository.
